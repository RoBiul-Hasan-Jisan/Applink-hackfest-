# Business model (hypotheses, not traction)
Free for residents (reporting, essential prevention information). Paying institutions fund coordination, audit and support: campus/hospital (SaaS), factory/construction sites (site licence + onboarding), NGOs (project contract), municipalities (partner pilot then procurement).
Six months (targets): Dec 2026 production readiness · Jan 2027 one real institutional pilot · Feb workflow assessment (review times, priority disagreements, actual resolution) · Mar approach first payer · Apr second site · May pricing/retention test.
Contribution margin = collected revenue − telecom charges − hosting − operations − field-service costs. Do not present assumed customers or revenue as traction; validate willingness to pay in real interviews. Respect existing initiatives (Mosquito Alert Bangladesh, DNCC Dengue Watch, municipal complaint channels): the claim is a verified action-tracking layer at an authorised site, not first-ever or government-endorsed.
